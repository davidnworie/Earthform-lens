Horton plot: segments per Strahler order

Strahler ordering of the channels extracted from the terrain (cells draining more than the threshold area). Rb = geometric mean of Nᵤ/Nᵤ₊₁ (typically 3–5); drainage density = total channel length ÷ catchment area. Values depend on the threshold and terrain resolution: compare rivers at the same settings.

Hypsometric curve

Water is routed over the terrain (depressions filled, steepest descent) and every cell draining to the river mouth is counted. Very large basins use coarser terrain (several hundred metres per cell).

Floodplain and valley width

At regular cross-sections the terrain is followed out from the channel on both sides until it rises more than the flood height (green: floodplain) or four times that (yellow: valley floor). Blue dots: channel width (measure widths in Channel geometry). Confinement = floodplain width ÷ channel width: below 2 confined, 2–10 partly confined, above 10 unconfined. ⓘ How to capture a clean, poster-ready image
  1. Hide labels. Google Earth: ☰ → Map Style → Clean. Google Maps: Layers → More → untick Labels.
  2. Frame for contrast. The strongest images split the frame between two worlds (city and forest, fields and road, water and land), or fill it with one repeating pattern.
  3. Go portrait. Hold the phone upright and zoom until the feature fills the screen. Earthform Lens crops to 5:7 or 4:5.
  4. Look straight down. Tilted 3D views distort shapes and measurements. In Google Earth press the 2D/3D button for a flat view.
  5. Screenshot and open it here. Earthform Lens trims the toolbars and buttons, applies the Atlas look and adds the place label.
  6. For measurements, use Earth's ruler on a feature you'll recognise, then enter the length in Lab → Setup.
  7. Credit imagery when sharing, e.g. “Imagery © Google, Airbus, Maxar”, or “Copernicus Sentinel‑2” for Sentinel images.

4 · Channel network

Measures the whole connected network that drains to the outlet: Strahler stream orders, Horton ratios, loops, channel density and how far land lies from the nearest channel.

Junctions & scaling

Tributaries & distributaries

Every branch that meets the main channel: tributaries flow in, distributaries flow out, side channels split and rejoin. Measures where each joins, its length, width and junction angle.

Rivers

Each network split into individual rivers: at every junction the river continues into the arm with the same water and the larger upstream network; other arms start new rivers. The largest are major rivers.

All channels in the image

Every channel-shaped piece of water, connected or not: its own length, width and sinuosity.

5 · Lakes & ponds

Measures every separate water body: size, shape and orientation. The size distribution and orientation of thaw lakes record permafrost processes and the prevailing winds.

6 · Bedforms & lineaments

Finds repeating patterns such as dune crests, ripples, ridges and fractures using a 2‑D Fourier spectrum and gradient orientations. Draw a box with ▦ Pattern over a uniform patch. Without a box, the analysis area is used.

7 · Surface cover

Groups pixels into colour classes (water, sand, vegetation, rock…) with k‑means, then measures how much area each covers and how fragmented it is.
